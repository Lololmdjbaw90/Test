2. Purpose and audience
Sugerly is a consumer social app for artists and art enthusiasts, aged 13+ or the higher local minimum. People share artwork and works in progress, receive feedback, discover creators, post reels, join communities and chat. Bunny customization and earned-coin rewards support personal expression.

3. Access and main features
Use the demo credentials in the private Sign-in Information fields (verified on 22 October 2026). No sample files or music subscription are required for the core social features.
Home shows discovery, posts and daily rewards. Community contains conversations; Fanart contains artwork. Open content to like, save or comment. Use its overflow menu to Report; a user's profile menu provides Block/Unblock.
The create (+) menu opens posts, artwork and reels. Import your own photos/video; edit text/timing and preview. Reels support original video audio and the user's own uploaded tracks. Chats and community servers are accessible from navigation/the menu. The GIF/sticker picker offers user-created media and local creation tools.
Menu > Customize bunny offers bodies, faces, clothing and colors. Creator items may be listed for earned coins. Menu > MimiPlus has optional earned-coin extras. The native app does not sell coins for real money or provide coin cash withdrawals.
Artists need admin portfolio approval to open commissions. Commission card checkout uses Stripe and is available only on the US storefront. Artist-only commission setup requires an approved artist account; the ordinary review account is not an admin.
Account deletion: Menu > Settings > Delete account, then confirm with the disposable account password. Please keep the supplied review account active.

4. External services
Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Storage and Cloud Functions provide accounts, content and backend APIs. Netlify hosts the website/fallback functions. APNs/PushKit and CallKit support incoming calls; WebRTC transports calls; ReplayKit supports screen broadcasts. Google/Cloudflare STUN and configured TURN relays support connectivity. Stripe processes eligible commission checkout. Google Gemini via Firebase supports applicable moderation/caption assistance. Optional Apple Music playback uses MusicKit with user authorization/subscription; it does not export Apple Music into reels.

5. Regional differences
Core social features are consistent across supported storefronts. Stripe commission checkout is restricted to the United States storefront. Optional Apple Music availability depends on Apple's region/subscription support. Deezer and GIPHY are disabled for every account and region in this release.

6. Third-party material
Deezer catalog browsing, previews, saved catalog playback and reel sound reuse are disabled pending authorization. GIPHY search and provider media loading are disabled pending integration review. Creating a developer key is not represented as a music/GIF rights clearance. Original uploads remain subject to ownership/permission requirements and moderation/reporting. Bundled fonts include OFL licenses; generated Earshot UI effects include an MIT license. Sugerly does not provide a regulated professional service.
