2. Purpose and audience
Sugerly is a consumer social app for artists and art enthusiasts aged 13 and older, subject to the higher minimum age applicable locally. Users share artwork and works in progress, receive feedback, discover creators, post reels, join communities and chat. Bunny customization and earned-coin rewards add personal expression.

3. Access and main features
Use the demo account in the private Sign-in Information fields; its credentials were successfully verified on 22 October 2026. Basic browsing does not require sample files or an Apple Music subscription.
Home shows artwork, posts, discovery and daily rewards. Community contains conversations; Fanart contains artwork. Open an item to like, save or comment; its overflow menu offers Report. A user profile's overflow menu offers Block/Unblock.
The create (+) menu opens posts, artwork and reels. Import your own photos/video, edit text/timing and preview. Chats and community servers are accessible from navigation/the menu.
Menu > Customize bunny offers bodies, faces, clothing and colors. Creator items can be listed for earned coins. Menu > MimiPlus offers optional extras unlocked with earned coins. The native app does not sell coins for real money; creator coins are not cash withdrawals.
Artists require admin portfolio approval before opening commissions. US-storefront commission card checkout uses Stripe; an approved demo artist account is needed to review artist-only screens.
Deletion: Menu > Settings > Delete account, then confirm using the disposable account password. Keep the reviewer account active.

4. External services
Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Storage and Cloud Functions provide accounts, content and backend APIs. Netlify hosts the website/fallback functions. Apple APNs/PushKit and CallKit handle incoming calls; WebRTC transports calls and ReplayKit supports screen broadcasts, with STUN/TURN relay support. Deezer provides music metadata/previews; optional Apple Music playback uses MusicKit; GIPHY provides GIFs/stickers. Stripe processes commission checkout. Google Gemini via Firebase provides applicable moderation/caption assistance. The configured email and relay providers must be confirmed before the final service list is submitted.

5. Regions
Core social navigation is the same across supported storefronts. Commission card checkout is enabled only on the United States storefront and is unavailable elsewhere. Music catalogs and provider content can vary by region and subscription. App Store Connect currently selects all 175 countries/regions, including mainland China; regional availability/compliance still needs a final review before release.

6. Protected material
Sugerly is a social/art app, not a regulated professional service. Users must own or have permission for uploads; report/block controls are provided. Bundled fonts have OFL licenses; procedural Earshot UI sounds include an MIT license. These do not establish distribution or synchronization rights for unrelated songs, artwork or GIF exports. Applicable provider authorizations/rights documents remain to be verified before making a rights declaration.
